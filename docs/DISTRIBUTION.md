# Distribution decision

Decision (2026-10-29): stay private. No Marketplace listing and no Technology Partner application yet.

| Route | When | Requirements | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Private app, direct install | Now through pilot | Hosted app, private registration | Next step after approval |
| Public app (installable by link) | After pilot shows 2 of 3 repeat use | `--public` registration, privacy/support pages live | Not yet |
| Free Marketplace listing | After public app is stable | [Listing requirements](https://docs.github.com/en/apps/github-marketplace/creating-apps-for-github-marketplace/requirements-for-listing-an-app): working app, landing/privacy/support, verified support email, logo and screenshots, Marketplace agreement | Copy drafted in `marketplace/LISTING.md` |
| Technology Partner program | Established commercial product with customers | Separate application | Not a requirement to ship |

Revisit after the pilot metrics in `docs/PILOT.md`.
